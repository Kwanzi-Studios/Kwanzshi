making markets

open one

created markets

!market create "Question?"
open ended. no clock. trading runs until you close or resolve it. yes starts at 50.
!market create "Question?" 20
same, with yes starting at 20 instead of 50.
!market create "Question?" 20 30
with a clock. after 30 minutes it settles itself: yes if a mod resolved it yes before time ran out, otherwise no, it didn't happen.

run it

!market resolve market2 yes
pays out a created market. yes or no. this is final, there's no undo once people are paid.
!market close market2
stops trading on a created market now. resolve it later.
!market void market2
cancels any market. everyone gets their KWONKS back.
