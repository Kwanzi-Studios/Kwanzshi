mods: making markets

mods make the markets, from chat, with !market. three kinds.

called markets. anything you can answer yes or no to later. !market create "New PB today?" 20 30 puts up the question with yes starting at 20 and a 30 minute clock. the title goes in quotes. the first number is the starting yes price (skip it and it's 50). the second is minutes until trading stops (skip it and it runs until you close it). add thin, normal or deep at the end to set how much is on the shelf at each price: thin moves fast, deep takes a lot to move. these don't settle themselves. when the answer is in, !market resolve market2 yes or no pays everyone out.

KWK markets. a bet on the rate. !market kwk above 1.05 in 30 min or !market kwk below 0.95 in 2 hours. these settle themselves by checking the actual number when the clock hits. nothing else to do.

kalshi mirrors. real markets with real prices, mirrored here. !market kalshi giants searches. it shows the best match, and !market kalshi create opens it. or paste a kalshi link. an event link with several markets under it gives you !market kalshi list to pick one by number, or !market kalshi create all to open every one. mirrors settle themselves when kalshi does.

the rules. ask something with one clear yes or no answer, and say when it's decided. resolve called markets as soon as the answer is in, people are waiting on their KWONKS. !market close market2 stops trading early but leaves it open to resolve later. if it turns out the question was broken, !market void market2 gives everyone back what they paid, no harm done. markets with a clock stop trading a minute before it runs out so nobody snipes the ending. every contract pays ₭₪100 when it's right, that part is never up to you.
