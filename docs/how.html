How to play

Three steps. If you can type in chat you can lose Kwonks with the best of them.

1. Get Kwonks

Redeem a Buy Kwonks channel point reward. Three sizes. Bigger packs are a better deal because I like commitment. Your first trade is on the house for ₭₪100, so you can also just skip this and bet.

2. Bet

Look at the overlay or the markets page. Every market has a name like market2 and a Yes price and a No price. That price is what one contract costs, and every contract pays ₭₪100 if you're right and nothing if you're wrong. Yes at 30 means you risk 30 to win 100. Yes at 90 is nearly free money, and I say nearly on purpose.

!buy market2 yes 50 puts 50 Kwonks on Yes. !buy market2 no 50 bets against it. Word order doesn't matter and m2 works too.

3. Cash out, or don't

!sell market2 sells everything you hold there at the current price. Up 40? Take it. Down 40? Also take it, honestly. Or hold to the end and get paid ₭₪100 a contract if you called it.

The other stuff

!p shows your balance and what you're holding. !top is the leaderboard. !kwk is the Kwonk rate. !shop is the shop.

What is KWK

KWK is what a Kwonk is worth. It moves every few seconds like a coin chart. It goes up when people buy packs, trade, and spend in the shop. It goes down when too many Kwonks pile up. Every hour there's a market on where it'll be at the top of the hour, and that one settles by checking the actual number. Nobody picks the answer. Not me, not a mod, not the bot.

The spread

Yes and No don't add up to 100. That gap is the spread. It's how the house eats. Buy big and the price moves against you as you go, because there's only so much on the shelf at each price. That's how real exchanges work and I'm not about to make it easier for you.
